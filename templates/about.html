about me

My story, briefly

I got into Computer Science because I genuinely love coding. I enjoy writing code, figuring out how things work, and finding different ways to solve a problem. What started as an interest in programming gradually became something I wanted to explore more seriously through Computer Science.

As a CSE student, most of my learning happens through a mix of classes, coding, projects, and experimenting with different technologies. I've explored Java, Python, web development, databases, AI, machine learning, and NLP, and I'm still discovering the areas I enjoy the most.

For me, coding isn't just about getting a program to work. I like the process of thinking through a problem, getting stuck, debugging it, and finally seeing it work. That's probably the part of Computer Science I enjoy the most.

Right now I'm especially interested in full-stack development and AI/ML, and I'm working toward building complete applications and exploring how AI can make them smarter.
